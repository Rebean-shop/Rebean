Our Story

From Coffee Beans to Candle Scents

ReBean was founded in 2025 with a passion for creating cozy atmospheres and the comforting aroma of coffee. We believe that every candle should tell a story of quality, craftsmanship, and care.

Our journey began when we discovered how the rich, complex notes of coffee could be transformed into captivating fragrances that transform any space into a warm sanctuary.
